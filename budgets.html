💡 Budgeting Tips for Students
🍽
50-30-20 Rule
50% needs, 30% wants, 20% savings. Adapt it to your student life.
📱
Track Daily
Log expenses as soon as you spend to avoid forgetting small purchases.
🛒
Plan Groceries
Buy in bulk and cook meals — you can save 40-60% over eating out daily.
🎯
Emergency Fund
Keep at least ₹2,000-₹5,000 set aside for unexpected expenses.
